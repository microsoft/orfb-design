# orfb-design

## Overview

orfb-design is a suite of MCP servers for designing and evaluating aqueous organic redox flow battery electrolytes.

### What can orfb-design do

orfb-design was developed to equip agents with tools to design and evaluate molecules for use as aqueous organic redox flow battery electrolytes.

A detailed discussion of orfb-design, including how it was developed and tested, can be found in our paper at: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02618>

### Intended uses

orfb-design is best suited for replicating the redox flow battery design work described in: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02618>

orfb-design is being shared with the research community to facilitate reproduction of our results and foster further research in this area.

orfb-design is intended to be used by domain experts who are independently capable of evaluating the quality of outputs before acting on them.

### Out-of-scope uses

orfb-design produces computational estimates. Outputs produced using orfb-design should be checked by a knowledgeable user prior to use. orfb-design does not evaluate toxicology, environmental impact, or process safety and should be used in concert with dedicated safety evaluation tools in any autonomous laboratory integration.

We do not recommend using orfb-design in commercial or real-world applications without further testing and development. It is being released for research purposes.

orfb-design was not designed or evaluated for all possible downstream purposes. Developers should consider its inherent limitations as they select use cases, and evaluate and mitigate for accuracy, safety, and fairness concerns specific to each intended downstream use.

Without further testing and development, orfb-design should not be used in sensitive domains where inaccurate outputs could suggest actions that lead to injury or negatively impact an individual's legal, financial, or life opportunities.

We do not recommend using orfb-design in the context of high-risk decision making (e.g. in law enforcement, legal, finance, or healthcare).

## How to get started

To begin using orfb-design, please follow the quick-start guide at <https://github.com/microsoft/orfb-design#quick-start>.

## Evaluation

orfb-design was evaluated on its ability to equip agents with tools to design and evaluate molecules for use as aqueous organic redox flow battery electrolytes.

A detailed discussion of our evaluation methods and results can be found in our paper at: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02618>

### Evaluation methods

We integrated orfb-design with the CLIO agent (<https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.02789>).

The model used for evaluation was GPT-5.2. For more on this specific model, please see <https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-5.2>.

Results may vary if orfb-design is used with a different model based on its unique design, configuration and training.

### Evaluation results

At a high level, we found that orfb-design enabled the CLIO agent to leverage the component tools in a molecular design workflow.

## Limitations

orfb-design was developed for research and experimental purposes. Further testing and validation are needed before considering its application in commercial or real-world scenarios.

orfb-design was designed and tested using the English language. Performance in other languages may vary and should be assessed by someone who is both an expert in the expected outputs and a native speaker of that language.

Outputs generated by AI may include factual errors, fabrication, or speculation. Users are responsible for assessing the accuracy of generated content. All decisions leveraging outputs of the system should be made with human oversight and not be based solely on system outputs.

orfb-design inherits any biases, errors, or omissions produced by its base model. Developers are advised to choose an appropriate base LLM/MLLM carefully, depending on the intended use case.

orfb-design uses the Graphormer model. See <https://github.com/microsoft/Graphormer> to understand the capabilities and limitations of this model.

orfb-design uses the SolTranNet model. See <https://github.com/gnina/SolTranNet> to understand the capabilities and limitations of this model.

orfb-design uses the RetroChimera model. See <https://github.com/microsoft/retrochimera> to understand the capabilities and limitations of this model.

orfb-design inherits any biases, errors, or omissions characteristic of its training data, which may be amplified by any AI-generated interpretations.

There has not been a systematic effort to ensure that systems using orfb-design are protected from security vulnerabilities such as indirect prompt injection attacks. Any systems using it should take proactive measures to harden their systems as appropriate.
